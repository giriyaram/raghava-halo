For families with school-age children, the school question comes before almost everything else in a residential decision. A great apartment in the wrong school catchment is still the wrong apartment.

Kondapur has one of the strongest school ecosystems in Hyderabad for families in the premium segment — a combination of proximity to several highly-regarded institutions, variety in curriculum boards, and access via short, manageable school runs.

Here is an honest guide to what is within reach.

## CHIREC International School

**Distance from Kondapur:** 3–5 minutes by car (approximately 2 km)

CHIREC is the school most frequently cited by Kondapur families when explaining why they chose the neighbourhood. It is an IB (International Baccalaureate) school offering PYP, MYP, and Diploma programme — the curriculum preferred by families with potential international mobility and those targeting global university admissions.

The school has a strong academic reputation and competitive admission. Seats are limited and the waitlist is real — families planning to enrol children should initiate the process well before the academic year they intend to join.

For parents at IT companies with possible international postings, CHIREC's IB curriculum offers continuity across countries — a meaningful consideration for children mid-schooling.

**Curriculum:** IB (PYP, MYP, Diploma)
**Best for:** Families considering international relocation, or those targeting IB-pathway university admissions

## Oakridge International School

**Distance from Kondapur:** 12–18 minutes (Bachupally campus) or 20 minutes (Financial District campus)

Oakridge offers both IB and Cambridge IGCSE curricula and is consistently ranked among Hyderabad's top international schools. The Financial District campus, in particular, is accessible from Kondapur via the Gachibowli corridor.

**Curriculum:** IB and Cambridge IGCSE
**Best for:** Families wanting curriculum flexibility or an alternative to CHIREC's competitive admissions

## Glendale Academy

**Distance from Kondapur:** 15–20 minutes (Uppal and Moula Ali campuses are further, but the Hyderabad central campus is accessible)

Glendale offers CBSE and is a mid-premium option for families who prefer the CBSE curriculum with good infrastructure and extracurricular programmes.

**Curriculum:** CBSE
**Best for:** Families committed to the CBSE pathway, particularly those whose children may relocate within India

## Eurokids and Preschool Options

For families with very young children, Kondapur and its immediate surrounds have multiple preschool options — Eurokids, Kidzee, and several independent play schools — within a 5-minute radius. This matters practically for the 2–5 age group when proximity and daily schedule flexibility are paramount.

## DPS (Delhi Public School)

**Distance from Kondapur:** 20–25 minutes (nearest campus at Mahendra Hills or Nacharam)

DPS is one of India's most recognised CBSE school brands. Hyderabad has multiple campuses, though none is immediately adjacent to Kondapur. For families with a strong preference for DPS specifically, the commute is manageable but not as convenient as the CHIREC option.

## What Parents Actually Prioritise

Based on conversations with families who have made the Kondapur choice, the school decision typically comes down to three things:

**Curriculum board:** IB families almost always end up at CHIREC or Oakridge. CBSE families have more options but slightly longer commutes.

**School run logistics:** A 5-minute school drop-off versus a 20-minute one may seem minor. At 5 AM wake-ups, 180 days a year, it is not. CHIREC's proximity to Kondapur is a genuine practical advantage — not just a marketing bullet.

**Sibling planning:** Families with younger children who may need to split between a primary and preschool often value having both options within a small radius. Kondapur's ecosystem of preschools alongside CHIREC for older children makes this manageable.

## The School Admission Reality

In Hyderabad's premium school segment, admission is increasingly competitive and often begins 1–2 years before the child's entry year. Parents seriously considering Kondapur as a home base should initiate enquiries with target schools simultaneously with their apartment search — not after possession.

Some families move to Kondapur specifically to establish local address credentials for preferred school applications. The residential address requirement is not always waived.

## Summary

For a family in the ₹1.5–3 Cr apartment segment seeking a Hyderabad home, Kondapur offers better school access per km than almost any other location in the IT corridor. CHIREC's IB programme within 5 minutes is the standout advantage. The broader ecosystem — multiple preschools, secondary options within 20 minutes, and Oakridge accessible for curriculum alternatives — makes the neighbourhood one of the strongest for families in Hyderabad's west zone.
