A project in the pre-RERA stage is being actively marketed before the developer has completed the regulatory filing required to register it as an official project. This is not illegal — RERA allows a window between initial marketing and formal registration — but it means buyers are making commitments based on developer representations rather than registered, publicly verifiable documents.

Here is a framework for evaluating a new launch before RERA registration is complete.

## Understand What Pre-RERA Means

When you book a pre-RERA project, you are booking based on:
- The developer's current representations (pricing, floor plans, specifications, timeline)
- The developer's reputation and track record
- Land ownership documents the developer holds
- Pre-application materials submitted or being submitted to TSRERA

What you do NOT yet have:
- A government-verified record of specifications and commitments
- A publicly searchable registration you can independently verify
- The statutory protection of the RERA delay compensation framework against a registered commitment

This is the risk. It is manageable with the right verification steps.

## Step 1: Verify Land Ownership

Before any other evaluation, confirm the developer actually owns (or has a valid development agreement for) the land on which the project will be built.

Ask for: Sale deed or development agreement, and the property survey number. With the survey number, you can verify ownership through the Telangana Dharani portal. A lawyer can do this verification for you in 2–3 days.

A developer who is reluctant to share land ownership documentation before taking a booking advance is a developer worth being cautious about.

## Step 2: Verify Building Plan Sanction

Has the developer obtained sanction for the building plan from HMDA (Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority) or GHMC (Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation)?

A sanctioned building plan is required before RERA registration can proceed. If the plan is not yet sanctioned, the RERA registration is correspondingly further away, and the specifications you are seeing in the brochure may still change.

Ask to see the approved building plan or the application number filed with HMDA/GHMC. Sanction status can be verified through the respective authority's online portal.

## Step 3: Assess the Developer's RERA Commitment

Ask directly: When do you expect to complete RERA registration? What is preventing registration now?

Legitimate answers: "We are waiting for building plan sanction, expected in X weeks" or "We are completing title documentation, expected by [date]."

Less legitimate answers: Vague assurances, deflection, or claims that "RERA doesn't apply to this project." All projects above 500 sq m or 8 apartments are required to register. There are no exceptions in Telangana for new launches above this threshold.

## Step 4: Evaluate the Developer's Track Record

A pre-RERA project is more dependent on developer trust than a registered project. This makes the track record evaluation more important, not less.

- Visit completed projects by the same developer
- Check their RERA compliance history on previous registered projects
- Ask for references from previous buyers in their completed projects
- Search for delivery timelines on previous projects

A developer with 10 successful, on-time deliveries is a fundamentally different pre-RERA proposition from a developer on their first or second project.

## Step 5: Structure the Booking Advance Carefully

In a pre-RERA project, the booking advance is made without the full protection of a registered commitment. Protect yourself through:

**Payment amount:** Keep the initial booking advance minimal — ₹1–5 lakh rather than a larger tranche. Do not make significant payments (above 10% of project value) until RERA registration is complete.

**Payment destination:** The booking advance must go to the developer's bank account (preferably the project escrow) — not to a channel partner or individual. Get a receipt from the developer.

**Conditions in the booking form:** Ask for a clause that specifies the project will be RERA registered by a stated date, and that you are entitled to a full refund of the advance if registration is not completed by that date. Many developers will agree to this.

## Step 6: Evaluate the Specifications Before They Are Locked

The specifications in a pre-RERA project are not yet frozen by law. However, the specifications a developer markets now are what they intend to register. Changes between pre-RERA marketing and RERA registration can occur — they should be minimal and should be disclosed.

When RERA registration is completed, verify the registered specifications against what you were shown during pre-launch. They should match. If there are material differences — in carpet area, in amenities, in specifications — you have grounds to renegotiate or withdraw.

## The Risk-Reward Balance

Pre-RERA projects typically offer the best pricing — before the price adjustments that come with formal launch and registration. For a buyer who has done thorough verification and is dealing with a developer with a strong track record, a pre-RERA booking at a meaningful price advantage is a reasonable decision.

For a buyer who has not completed the verification steps above, a pre-RERA booking is a trust decision rather than an informed one. The difference matters.
