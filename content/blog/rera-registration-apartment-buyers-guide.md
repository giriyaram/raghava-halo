RERA — the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 — is the most significant consumer protection legislation in Indian real estate history. It changed the relationship between developers and buyers fundamentally. Yet most buyers who encounter the term have only a vague sense of what it actually means for them.

Here is a plain-language explanation of what RERA registration means, what it protects, and what questions to ask before booking in a pre-RERA project.

## What RERA Registration Actually Is

When a developer registers a project under RERA, they submit a detailed package to the state authority — in Telangana, this is TSRERA (Telangana State Real Estate Regulatory Authority). This package includes:

- Sanctioned building plans and floor plans
- Project specifications and unit details
- The committed possession date
- The developer's financial information and track record
- Land title documents
- Construction timeline milestones

Once registered, this information becomes public. Any buyer can visit the TSRERA portal (tsrera.telangana.gov.in) and look up the project — seeing the actual committed specifications, the possession date on record, and any complaints or compliance issues.

## What Changes When a Project Is RERA Registered

**Accountability:** The developer is legally bound to the specifications and timeline they submitted. If they deviate from the floor plans or specifications, or delay possession beyond the committed date, you have formal legal recourse.

**Transparency:** You can verify what was promised versus what is delivered, using the public RERA record as the reference document. This is important because verbal assurances during the sales process are not enforceable — registered commitments are.

**Escrow protection:** RERA requires developers to deposit a percentage of project funds into an escrow account that can only be used for that specific project's construction. This reduces — though does not eliminate — the risk of funds being diverted to other projects.

**Compensation for delay:** If the developer delays possession beyond the committed date, RERA provides a framework for buyers to claim interest on amounts paid, without having to approach the courts directly.

## What Buying in a Pre-RERA Project Means

A project in the pre-registration phase — which is common for new launches that are building out documentation before RERA filing — carries different risks that buyers should understand clearly.

The specifications, floor plans, pricing, and possession date you are shown at the time of booking are not yet on public record. They are the developer's current representations. Until RERA registration, there is no state authority enforcing them.

This does not mean a pre-RERA project is necessarily risky — many reputable developers offer pre-registration bookings and subsequently register exactly as described. It means you are relying on the developer's track record and integrity more than on regulatory enforcement.

**What to ask in a pre-RERA project:**
- When is RERA registration expected?
- What will the registered possession date be?
- Can you provide a copy of the sanctioned plan that has been submitted or will be submitted to TSRERA?
- What is the developer's RERA compliance history on previous projects? (Verifiable on the portal)

## How to Check TSRERA for Any Hyderabad Project

The TSRERA portal allows you to search by project name or developer name. For registered projects, you can see:

- The project registration number (useful to quote in all correspondence)
- The committed possession date
- The project specifications as registered
- Whether there are any registered complaints against the developer
- Compliance status — whether construction milestone reports have been filed

This takes five minutes and should be a standard step before booking any Hyderabad apartment. The information is public and free to access.

## RERA and the Brochure: What to Cross-Reference

Once a project is RERA registered, compare the registered floor plan with the one in the brochure you were shown. They should match. If the RERA floor plan shows a different layout, smaller rooms, or different specifications from the marketing material, you have found a discrepancy worth raising before you sign.

Similarly, compare the RERA registered price per sft with the price you were quoted. Minor differences exist (registration fees, GST treatment, etc.), but significant gaps between quoted and registered pricing deserve explanation.

## The Practical Bottom Line

RERA registration is a meaningful signal of a project's readiness and a developer's seriousness. It is also your primary legal protection mechanism if something goes wrong.

For any project you are considering booking, ask directly: is this project RERA registered? If yes, get the registration number and verify it independently on the TSRERA portal. If not yet registered, get a committed timeline for registration and ask what specifications will be submitted.

The documentation is public. Use it.
