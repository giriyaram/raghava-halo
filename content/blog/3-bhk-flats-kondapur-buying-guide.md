Kondapur is the most practical premium residential address in Hyderabad for IT professionals. It's close enough to work, fully developed as a neighbourhood, and now home to a new generation of high-rise residential projects that bring vertical living to a market that has historically been dominated by low-rise gated communities.

If you're evaluating 3 BHK flats in Kondapur, this checklist covers what to look at, in roughly the order it matters.

## 1. Location Connectivity — Be Specific About Travel Time

"Close to Financial District" means different things depending on traffic, time of day, and which gate of the Financial District you actually need to access. Before deciding a location is convenient, drive it during your typical work hours — not on a Sunday afternoon.

The Kondapur–Financial District drive is typically 15–20 minutes during off-peak hours. During morning rush (8–9:30 AM), it can extend to 25–35 minutes. That's still reasonable, but know what you're buying.

Also check: ORR access for those who travel to the airport frequently, and proximity to the specific school your children attend or will attend.

## 2. Builder Track Record

In Hyderabad's premium segment, builder reputation matters significantly. Look for:

- **Delivered projects:** Has the builder delivered comparable projects on time? Not just completed projects, but comparable scale and specification.
- **RERA history:** Are their previous projects registered and compliant? Absence of RERA issues is a positive signal.
- **Post-possession maintenance:** Speak to residents of their previous projects if you can. Maintenance quality after possession is a better predictor of the builder's long-term intentions than anything in the brochure.

## 3. RERA Registration

Non-negotiable. Confirm the project's RERA registration before anything else. In Telangana, this is verifiable on the RERA TS portal. The registration number should appear on all marketing materials.

RERA registration doesn't eliminate risk, but it creates accountability for delivery timelines, specifications, and refund obligations that don't exist without it.

## 4. Lifts Per Floor — The Number That Predicts Daily Frustration

As noted in more detail in our piece on high-rise living, the lift-to-apartment ratio is the single most predictive number for everyday livability in a high-rise.

For a 3 BHK buyer at the ₹1.6–2.5 Cr range, you're typically looking at towers with 4–8 apartments per floor. The questions to ask:

- How many lifts does the tower have?
- Are lifts shared across all floors or dedicated to certain floor ranges?
- What is the peak-hour lift wait time in comparable projects from the same builder?

A tower with 8 apartments per floor should have at least 4 lifts. 8 lifts is better. 2 lifts for 8 apartments per floor is a known livability problem.

## 5. Amenity Placement and Square Footage

Ask two specific questions:

**Where are the amenities?** Stilt level (accessible directly from the lobby) or higher floors (requiring a separate lift ride)? This affects frequency of use more than any other single factor.

**What is the total clubhouse area?** "30+ amenities" can mean 30,000 sq ft or 90,000 sq ft. The difference is significant. A 90,000 sq ft clubhouse genuinely accommodates 1,000+ families. A 15,000 sq ft clubhouse listed as having "world-class amenities" will feel crowded within 6 months of possession.

## 6. Floor Plan Efficiency

Ask for the architectural floor plan, not the rendered image. Look for:

- **Corridor length:** How much of the apartment is consumed by internal circulation? Less is better.
- **Bedroom size:** Are the secondary bedrooms functional (10x12 minimum) or token? 
- **Kitchen ventilation:** Does the kitchen have an external window, or is it a closed box?
- **Master bedroom:** Does it have a dedicated attached bathroom and a meaningful window/balcony?
- **Balcony placement:** Are balconies accessible from living areas, or awkwardly placed off service areas?

The master bedroom balcony in particular is worth asking about specifically — it's a feature that significantly affects the feel of the home and is not universal across all projects at this price point.

## 7. Vastu Compliance

If vastu matters to your household, ask specific questions (see our detailed piece on vastu in high-rises). The key points: tower orientation, entrance direction, kitchen placement.

If vastu doesn't matter to you personally, it still affects resale — so it's worth checking even from a pure investment perspective.

## 8. Possession Timeline — Is It Realistic?

A high-rise project of 50+ floors and 1,000+ homes requires a minimum of 4–5 years from construction commencement to possession. Projects promising 2–3 year delivery timelines on this scale should be scrutinised carefully.

Mid-2030 for a project launching in mid-2025 is a 5-year timeline — realistic for a project of this scale. Factor the timeline into your financing plan: if you're taking a home loan, understand when possession-linked tranches will be due.

## 9. Payment Plan Structure

In the ₹1.6–2.5 Cr range for a pre-launch or new launch project, most builders offer construction-linked payment plans. Understand:

- What percentage is due at booking versus at possession?
- Is the payment plan linked to construction milestones or calendar dates?
- What are the penalties for delayed possession?
- What is the refund policy if you need to exit before possession?

These terms vary significantly and have real financial implications over a 5-year horizon.

## 10. Resale Potential in Kondapur

Kondapur's fundamentals as a residential market are strong. The infrastructure is built, the employers are established, and the neighbourhood is not going to "develop" in a way that changes the character significantly — it's already developed.

For resale potential, the key factors are: builder brand, project specification quality, floor and view (in a high-rise, higher floors with open views command significant premiums), and vastu compliance. A well-planned 3 BHK in a quality high-rise in Kondapur bought at launch pricing has historically outperformed comparable low-rise apartments in the area over a 5–7 year holding period.

The checklist above helps you identify which projects fall into the "quality" category — and which are marketing a lifestyle that the specifications don't actually support.
